# Study materials

Study folders match the data and figure folders. Each contains an archived registration or OSF registration transcript and its source link. Supplementary study folders also contain the supplied survey PDFs.

The main-study `materials.md` files transcribe the procedures and measures from the working manuscript. They describe the available materials and are not original survey exports. For Studies 3a and 3b, they identify the earlier materials used and the added mediator questions or eligibility criteria.

Complete original main-study surveys and executable implementations were not available, so there are no live experiment previews. Original main exports, independent manipulation-check coding/adjudication records, the 40 excluded Main Study 3a records and Supplementary Study 1 thematic coding were also unavailable. Main Study 1’s registration and manuscript differ in some item wording and response-format details; resolving those differences requires the original survey and author confirmation.
