The customer-service policy, vignettes and behavioral measures follow [Main Study 2](../../study2/materials.md).

Procedure, Vignettes, and Measures

The procedure, vignettes, and measures in Study 3a were identical to Study 2, except for the addition of two items to measure our mediators. After indicating their likelihood of engaging in sabotage and de-escalation behaviors, participants were asked two questions: “To what extent do you agree with this statement: The reason I chose my answers was because I felt…” (1) “the customer deserved what I chose” and (2) “a sense of obligation to the employer.” Participants responded via a 7-point scale (1=Strongly disagree, 7=Strongly agree). These measures were repeated for each vignette. The “deservingness” and “obligation” variables were created by taking the average of the scale responses across the three vignettes per behavior type. Thus, we measured deservingness of sabotage, deservingness of de-escalation, obligation to refrain from or commit sabotage, and obligation to de-escalate.
