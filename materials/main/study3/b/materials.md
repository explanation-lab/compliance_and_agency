# Main Study 3b materials

The following description is transcribed from the working manuscript. Complete original survey exports were not available. This is a manuscript description, not an executable survey or original survey export.

The customer-service policy, vignettes, behavioral measures and mediator questions follow [Main Study 3a](../a/materials.md).

Procedure

Study 3b was identical to Study 3a, except for the sampling method. Participants were required to be full-time customer facing employees, working approximately 40 hours a week and regularly communicating directly with customers. The new eligibility criteria resulted in 100% of the sample consisting of current full-time customer-facing employees (i.e., answered “yes” to the question, “Are you customer-facing in your current job?”). In the final sample, 95.4% of the participants reported they were moderately, significantly or very experienced “working in customer service” (i.e., at or above the midpoint of the scale). The majority of participants, 57.1%, reported they were very experienced (i.e., the highest point on the scale). The median number of years participants had worked in their current job was 6 years (M = 8.2, SD = 7.5). Therefore, the sample contains employees who have provided many years of direct customer service, and the findings of this study are relevant for the population of service employees.
